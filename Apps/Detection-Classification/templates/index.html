Resource & Time Warning

Processing the full Bangkok CCTV repository (~61,000+ high-resolution frames across 100 cameras) performs continuous deep neural network inference (YOLO11). On GPU (CUDA), this takes approximately 25–40 minutes; on CPU it may take several hours.

What happens during this pipeline?
  1. Batch YOLO Inference: Detects & counts vehicles (car, motorcycle, bus, truck, bicycle) with temporal metadata.
  2. Bronze Lakehouse Ingestion: Stores raw structured predictions into bronze.vehicle_counts_raw with audit hash & timestamp.
  3. Silver Lakehouse Transformation: Types, cleans, validates, and deduplicates records into silver.vehicle_counts.
